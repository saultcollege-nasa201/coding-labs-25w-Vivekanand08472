Welcome to SaultRadioWaves.com

This website talks about some interesting facts about three different types of electromagnetic waves

  • Microwaves
  • X-Rays
  • Gamma Rays


Microwaves

Microwaves were accidentally discovered when a scientist's chocolate bar melted in his pocket during radar experiments

X-Rays

X-rays are so powerful that they can travel through the vacuum of space, helping astronomers study distant stars and black holes

Gamma Rays

Gamma rays are so powerful that they can actually break chemical bonds, which is why they are used in sterilizing medical equipments (and even food)
